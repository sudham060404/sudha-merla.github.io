Research Experience

15/05/2025 – 24/07/2025

Summer Research Fellow — BRIC-Institute for Stem Cell Science and Regenerative Medicine (InStem)

Advisors: Prof. Praveen Kumar Vemula and Dr. Tina Mukherjee

Project: Developing an in vivo model to study intestinal stem cell activation.

Developed a Drosophila model to evaluate the effects of a novel drug on intestinal stem cell proliferation and gut homeostasis using feeding assays, gut dissections, confocal imaging, and quantitative analysis. The treatment altered lifespan and stem cell activity, indicating systemic and gut-specific effects.

10/04/2023 – Current

Research Intern — SynTechBio Solutions Lab, Dayananda Sagar University

Advisors: Dr. Pronama Biswas and Dr. BM Ashwin Desai

Project 1: Electrospinning of biopolymers for production of nanofibers for biomedical applications

  • Extraction of polymers from okra mucilage and ashgourd to use as a stabiliser in the fabrication of PVA-based nanofibers via electrospinning, reducing the amount of synthetic PVA required.
  • Extraction of phytochemicals from Moringa oleifera leaf extracts to increase the functionality of the PVA nanofibers for wound healing applications.

Project 2: Identifying novel inhibitors via computational approaches such as molecular docking, molecular dynamics, and machine learning

  • Studied the effect of phytochemicals present in Carica papaya leaves by identifying their mechanism of inhibition against cancer proteins.
  • Identified potential drug combinations that can synergistically inhibit the BACE1 protein as a potential treatment for Alzheimer's disease.
  • Identified novel inhibitors of CXCR7 by employing machine learning and molecular docking.

Project 3: Meta-analysis of transcriptomic datasets to identify key genes

  • Identified differentially expressed genes associated with skin aging, distinguishing genes specifically involved in intrinsic vs. extrinsic aging.
